The Importance of Education
  READING PASSAGE

Education is the key to success in life. It opens doors to new opportunities and helps people develop critical thinking skills. Through education, individuals can pursue their dreams and contribute meaningfully to society. Moreover, education promotes equality and social justice by providing everyone with the chance to improve their lives.
